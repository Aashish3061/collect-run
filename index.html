Concept · Razorpay Payments app

Collect Run

Wholesalers sell on 15–30 days' credit and chase overdue dues by phone. Collect Run turns the Tally dues list into one owner-approved collection run, tracks the payments Razorpay confirms, and keeps everything else visibly unconfirmed.
